# AgentBond — Pitch Video Script (≤ 2 minutes)

**Target:** Colosseum Frontier 2026 judges
**Length:** 1:50 hard cap (leaves 10s buffer)
**Format:** Face on camera. Plain background. Phone or laptop webcam is fine. Good light > good camera.
**Tone:** Confident, direct, technical-credible. No filler. No "uh." No "so basically." Cut every soft word in editing.

> The judges said *"nothing fancy required. We're interested in how you think and communicate."* That means: clear thinking, sharp sentences, and one concrete reason to bet on you. Don't perform — explain.

---

## Before you hit record

- Wear something solid color (not white, not patterned). Black tee works.
- Window light 45° to your face. Not behind you.
- Phone propped at eye level, not below. Lock orientation.
- Do 3 takes. The 3rd one is always the best. Don't ship take 1.
- Speak 10% slower than feels natural. Pauses look confident on camera.

---

## Script

### [0:00 – 0:12] — Open
*Look at the lens. Not the screen. The lens.*

> "I'm **[YOUR NAME]**. I'm building **AgentBond** — economic trust for AI agents on Solana."

*Beat. One second of silence. Don't rush.*

---

### [0:12 – 0:45] — The problem (why this exists)

> "Here's the bet I'm making.
>
> AI agents are about to move serious money. They're already executing trades, managing wallets, running payments. Solana alone is processing fifteen million agent-driven transactions.
>
> But there's a gap nobody has filled: when an agent fails, the user has **zero recourse**. No refund. No slashing. No accountability. Agent reputation lives in Discord bios — trivially fabricated. The entire agentic economy runs on vibes."

---

### [0:45 – 1:10] — The solution (what you're building)

> "AgentBond fixes this with one primitive: **stake to serve**.
>
> Agents post collateral on-chain before they touch a job. Users escrow the reward. If the agent delivers, the contract pays them. If they fail, the contract slashes their stake automatically — and refunds the user.
>
> No arbitration. No appeals. No trust required. It's Proof-of-Stake, but for AI agents."

---

### [1:10 – 1:35] — Why me (the credibility punch — **REWRITE THIS IN YOUR OWN VOICE**)

> "Why am I the person building this?
>
> *[Pick the 2–3 sharpest things that are true about you. Examples — adapt these:]*
> - *"I've been writing Solana programs since [year]. I shipped [X]."*
> - *"I've spent the last [N] months running production AI agents — I've watched them lose money, and I've watched users have no way to fight back."*
> - *"I built the full protocol — Anchor program, SDK, API, frontend, six bots, an elizaOS plugin, and an MCP server — solo, in [N] weeks. It's live on devnet right now."*
> - *"I'm not waiting for someone else to fix this. The market is moving."*

> *Close this section with one sentence that lands. Example:*
> "I'm shipping the infrastructure I wish existed when I lost my first agent to a silent failure."

---

### [1:35 – 1:50] — Close

> "The agentic economy is fifty billion dollars by 2027. Right now it has no trust layer.
>
> AgentBond is the first. I checked — across 5,400 Colosseum projects, nothing comes close.
>
> Live on devnet. Program deployed. Fifteen sponsor integrations. Let's build."

*Hold eye contact for one beat after the last word. Then stop recording.*

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Memorize the first and last 10 seconds.** Read the middle if you have to — but the open and close must come out clean.
2. **Don't read off-camera.** If you need a teleprompter, use a free phone app (`Teleprompter Premium` or `PromptSmart Lite`) and stick the phone right next to the lens.
3. **Cut every "um," every "like," every "so basically."** Even one tells judges you're not sure.
4. **One sentence per breath.** If a sentence runs out of air, it's too long — rewrite it.
5. **Background should be one solid plane** — a blank wall, bookshelf, or curtain. No bed visible. No mess.

---

## Post-production

- Trim opening dead air aggressively — first frame should be you mid-word, not silence.
- Add a one-frame title card at 0:00: *"AgentBond — [Your Name] — Colosseum Frontier 2026"*
- Export 1080p, 30fps, MP4
- Upload to YouTube **unlisted** (not private — judges need to be able to open it)
- Title: `AgentBond — Pitch — Colosseum Frontier 2026`
- Paste link into the **Pitch Video** field on the submission form
