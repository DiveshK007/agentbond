# AgentBond — Pitch Video Script (60 seconds)

**Target:** Colosseum Frontier 2026 judges
**Length:** 60 seconds max
**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover (no face required)
**Tone:** Direct. No filler. Every sentence earns its place.

---

## What to have open on screen before recording:
- Tab 1: http://localhost:3000 (landing page with live stats)
- Tab 2: http://localhost:3000/leaderboard
- Tab 3: Solana Explorer — your slashing transaction URL
- Tab 4: http://localhost:3000/jobs

---

## Script

**[0–8s] — Show landing page, stat cards counting up**

> "AI agents are moving real money. When they fail, users lose with no recourse.
> AgentBond fixes this with one primitive: stake to serve."

**[8–18s] — Switch to /jobs, show jobs with escrowed SOL**

> "Users post jobs. Rewards are locked in a Solana smart contract — not held by us, not held by the agent. Locked. Until delivery."

**[18–35s] — Switch to Solana Explorer, show the slashing transaction**

> "This is FailBot. It took a job, submitted garbage, and got disputed.
> Watch what happens — [pause 2s] — the contract slashes its stake automatically.
> No human arbitration. No appeals. The code runs."

**[35–45s] — Switch to /leaderboard, show FailBot's failed count and slashed SOL**

> "Every agent's reputation is on-chain. Stake, completed jobs, failure count —
> all live, all verifiable. You can't fake this."

**[45–53s] — Show the elizaOS plugin code snippet (2 lines)**

> "Any elizaOS agent joins the protocol in two lines.
> [show code] One plugin, instant economic accountability."

**[53–60s] — Back to landing page, stats showing real numbers**

> "AgentBond. Live on Solana Devnet. Program deployed.
> Colosseum Frontier 2026."

---

## Recording Tips

1. Use QuickTime (Mac: Cmd+Shift+5) or OBS for screen recording
2. Record audio separately and sync if your mic is poor
3. Do the FailBot demo FIRST (bash scripts/run-failbot.sh) — save the Explorer URL
4. Open all 4 tabs before hitting record
5. Keep cursor movement slow and deliberate
6. Aim for 55 seconds — leave 5s buffer for submission form word count

---

## After Recording

- Trim dead air at start/end
- Export at 1080p minimum
- Upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Loom
- Paste URL into Colosseum submission form's "Demo Video" field
